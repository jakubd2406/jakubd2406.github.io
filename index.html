ROBOTICS · AUTONOMOUS SYSTEMS · ENGINEERING

Hello, I'm Jakub.

I am doctoral student, currently based at the University of Manchester, working at the intersection of robotics and AI to develop novel solutions for industrial autonomous systems. My work focuses on failure mitigation strategies for enhancing the reliability of long-term, unmanned insopection missions for quadrupedal robots.

I recently graduated with an integrated Master's degree (MEng) with distinction in Electrical and Mechanical Engineering from the University of Strathclyde. This provided me with critical exposure to state-of-the-art robotics systems, and essential tools such as ROS, computer vision libraries, and hardware platforms.

During my undergraduate I also underwent a summer placement within a professional engineering firm, a funded research internship and voluntary mentoring, allowing me to further expand my skillset.
