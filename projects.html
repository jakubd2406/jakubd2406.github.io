ENGINEERING / 01

Autonomous remanufacturing of end-of-life robots

5th year Master's projectROS2Computer visionMachine learning

One third of my Master's accreditation was attributed to this project, which was themed around the autonomous remanufacturing of end-of-life robots. The goal was to create an autonomous system for disassembly and reassembly tasks based on a UR5e industrial manipulator platfrom. My role focused on object recognition and localisation of standard industrial components such as screw fasteners, panels and gear-belts. The final deliverable achieved a distinction grade.

ENGINEERING / 02

Robotic ultrasonic inspection of industrial welds

Research internshipRoboticsNon-destructive testing

During my summer 2025 research internship at the Centre for Ultrasonic Engineering, I carried out a literature review in the field of autonomous non-destructive testing (NDT) and developed a proof-of-concept robotic system for ultrasonic inspection of geometrically complex industrial welds. The final documentation provided the university with material for a future journal publication.
