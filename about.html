My undergraduate degree was dual accredited by the the Institute of Electrical and Electronic Engineers (IEEE) and the Institute of Mechanical Engineers (IMechE), with taught components shared across the electrical and electronic (EEE) and mechanical and aerospace (MAE) engineering departments at the University of Strathclyde.

The foundational years (1-3) covered fundamental theory in electrical powers systems, electronics, programming, CAD design, fluid dynamics and structural engineering.

In years 4-5 my curriculum was tailored towards autonomous systems, with module selections in machine learning, robotics and embedded programming.

02PhD

PhD in Robotics and AI, EPSRC RAINZ CDT
University of Oxford
Expected graduation: 2030
Project Title: Predicting and Mitigating Robot and AI Failures in Long-Term Unmanned Inspection Missions in Industrial Settings

The theme of my research is the development of predictive models to anticipate and mitigate failures for quadrepedal robots operating within industrial environments. Unmanned inspection missions are crucial for ensuring safety and efficiency of critical assets, and require robust and reliable recovery mechanisms. This project integartes elements of navigation, mapping and locomotion to achieve reliable and flexible operation of complex systems.

The RAINZ CDT is a UK Research and Innovation Training Centre, and is a partnership between the University of Manchester, the University of Oxford and the University of Glasgow.
